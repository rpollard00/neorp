I have too much ADHD to think of something that I want to write about, and then remember to write about it. I worked on a mobile composer interface with Opus 5.5. It deploys to my Unraid server and also works offline once added to my phone. 

It seems trivial, but this whole thing is a static site, so a new post is a new deployment. My mobile composer lets me preview and ship posts like I am posting on Twitter.

I can hit it via Tailscale, even when I’m out of the house. In the pre-AI days this composer would not have been worth building, but now there’s basically no friction to building something like this. Anyway — first post from the composer.
